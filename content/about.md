# About Us

We are in the business of forming great partnerships that lead to shared, long-term success. As a trusted private equity firm, we provide counsel and insight to unlock growth for your company and the people within it.

**Partnership Approach**

We are partners in your success. Our approach is constructive, not instructive. From small steps to big ideas, what you do matters and we are here to invest in your vision. With our advice and capital, we help you drive growth opportunities in a way that makes sense for you.

**Our Focus**

The common thread our partners share is their belief that talent and a thriving culture drive success. We invest in well-positioned companies that are as passionate about human capital as we are. We seek to provide the attention and expertise to help take your company further. Together, we seek to create successful outcomes for our collective stakeholders.

## Our Sectors

**Industrial**
Manufacturing and value-added distribution are cornerstones of our economy. We back companies that create competitive advantage by providing discernible value to their end customers.

**Consumer**
A deep understanding of your customers can create even deeper brand bonds. We appreciate consumer companies that recognize the value of the customer and create unique products to meet their needs.

**Business Services**
Financial technology. Human capital management. Logistics. Outsourcing. We recognize that these services are critical to the core operations of the companies that utilize them.

### What We Believe In

We believe human capital is the single greatest asset of any company. When a culture thrives, the business thrives.
