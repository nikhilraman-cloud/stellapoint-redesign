## 4. How we work — what "culture first" means in practice

**Heading:** Culture is at Our Core

Above all else, we value partnership, integrity and a people-first mentality. We are dedicated to cultivating a culture of transparency, growth and opportunity.

That work starts before we invest. We assess human capital as part of every value-creation thesis, bring in a third party to interview management and understand team dynamics, and evaluate culture through metrics we track over time. Our communication is open and transparent — the kind that builds a productive relationship with management and the right team for the long term.

**Three short proof points (optional, set as a row of three):**

1. **Before we invest** — Human capital is assessed as part of the value-creation thesis, not after it.
2. **A like-minded partner** — Owners choose us for the same reason we choose them: a shared view of what makes a team work.
3. **Measured, not assumed** — Culture is evaluated with data and tracked as the company grows.

Source: first paragraph is live page, unchanged. Second paragraph and proof points are new, drawn from slide 35 ("Assess human capital management in development of value creation thesis", "Hire 3rd party consultant to interview management…", "Evaluate culture through metrics-based analysis and track evolution over time", "Invest in process of choosing a like-minded partner") and slide 5 ("Communication is open, transparent, and conducive to building a productive relationship with management and developing the right team for success").
